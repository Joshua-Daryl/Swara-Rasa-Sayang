Behind the Scenes

The Making of "He's Alive"

01

Songwriting

This Easter-themed album captures the journey of the Lord's Supper, Good Friday, the death, and resurrection of Jesus. It consists of 8 hymns directly inspired by Bible verses, and 2 gospel songs born from personal faith experiences and life's struggles.

02

Arrangement & Preparation

The creative process continued with music arrangement, demo recording, and careful selection of supporting artists to match the vocal character required by each song.

03

Rehearsals & Recording

After intensive rehearsals and material preparation, the recording was executed over one full week (in 2 daily shifts) at Artstudio Recording.

04

Mixing & Mastering

The final stage of music production — mixing and mastering — was carried out meticulously in collaboration with professionals to produce the highest audio quality.

05

Global Release

Working with a licensed international music aggregator (TuneCore), this album was distributed globally to 150 digital music platforms across 200 countries.
