Why We Are Here

GKJ Grogol Jakarta

A Fellowship Growing in Faith

The Javanese Christian Church (GKJ) Grogol Jakarta is a congregation rooted in faith, ministry, and love. Within this community, the voices and hearts of its members are united through worship and service.

Swara Rasa Sayang

Harmony That Touches the Heart

A community of GKJ Grogol Jakarta members who dedicate their voices to the ministry of praise, spreading love and bringing joy born from sincere faith.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Vision

Touching Souls, Spreading Love

To become a ministry that touches souls, spreads the love of Jesus Christ, and lifts praises for His glory, so that every song brings hope and inspires more people to know His love.

Mission

Proclaiming God's Love Through Worship

To proclaim the love of Jesus Christ through praise and worship, so that more people may hear, know, and experience His love, and be drawn closer to God in fulfilling the Great Commission.
